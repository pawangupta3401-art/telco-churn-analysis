Data-Driven Strategic Retention Playbook

Based on rigorous exploratory data analysis of 7,043 accounts and Power BI cohort insights, these four high-impact initiatives address 85%+ of churn drivers.

P1 • Service Quality

Fiber Optic Plan Overhaul

Fiber optic customers show a 41.9% churn rate (1,297 lost accounts). High rates correlate with high monthly bills ($78-$98) and technical support dissatisfaction.

  • Deploy automated network line quality monitoring for fiber customers in months 1-3.
  • Offer tiered speed stability guarantees and proactive technician outreach on fiber tickets.
  • Bundle complimentary Security Suite to balance high pricing sentiment.
P2 • Payment Channels

Electronic Check to Auto-Pay Incentive

Electronic check users exhibit 45.3% churn (1,071 lost accounts), whereas Bank Transfer / Credit Card auto-pay users churn at only 15.2% - 16.7%.

  • Launch a "$15 One-Time Bill Credit" for switching to automated ACH or credit card payments.
  • Promote recurring auto-pay discount ($5/month off) on next billing statement.
  • Introduce instant failure notifications and SMS payment retry reminders.
P3 • Contract Lock-In

Month-to-Month Migration Program

Month-to-month contracts represent 88.5% of all churn (1,655 accounts lost at 42.7% rate), while 2-Year contracts maintain an astonishing 97.2% retention rate (only 2.8% churn).

  • Target 3-6 month tenure customers with a "Lock Your Price for 12 Months" retention campaign.
  • Provide free premium streaming add-on or speed bump for entering a 1-year agreement.
  • Waive installation fees and equipment rental on contract conversions.
P4 • Feature Bundling

Security & Tech Support Onboarding

Less than 17% of churned users had Tech Support or Online Security. Customers with both services show an 85%+ retention rate.

  • Provide 90 days of free Tech Support and Online Security during new customer onboarding.
  • Train Level-1 support agents to attach free security trials whenever an administrative ticket is filed.
  • Create 1-click self-service security activation in the customer portal.
